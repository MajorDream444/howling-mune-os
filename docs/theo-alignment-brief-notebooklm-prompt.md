# Howling MUNE OS — Theo Alignment Brief + NotebookLM Podcast Prompt

Prepared by Major Dream Williams / AMA-Hanzo Systems

## Purpose

This document helps Theo understand the direction of the Howling MUNE OS, what has already been created, what is needed from him now, and how to stay clear, supportive, and transparent during the next stage of the build.

This is not just a website project. The direction has evolved into a living operating system for Howling MUNE: a client journey system, a visual vault, and a clean asset/workspace layer that allows Theo's work to become easier to enter, easier to organize, and easier to grow without losing its intimacy.

## Core Diagnosis

The medicine exists. The pathway around the medicine does not yet exist clearly enough.

Theo already has depth, visual authority, emotional intelligence, and lived philosophy. The work does not need to be made artificial or overly commercial. It needs structure around it: a clear front door, a way to receive serious inquiries, a way to understand client readiness, a place to organize photography and creative assets, and a pathway that helps aligned people enter the work without scattered conversations.

## Project Pillars

### 1. Client Journey System

The pathway for people who want to work with Theo. It includes the landing page, intake/application flow, client readiness questions, routing logic, CRM structure, follow-up rhythm, and eventual review/reporting layer.

### 2. Visual Vault

The archive and monetization layer for Theo's photography and image-based work. The system should prepare for photography collections, licensing inquiries, private image review, editorial use, future photography lessons, visual identity case studies, and possible digital products.

## What We Need From Theo

### Website Language

- What exact words feel true for the homepage?
- What phrases should we avoid?
- Does “Giving you back to you” still feel right?
- Does “Return to the self beneath the noise” feel aligned?
- What should people understand within the first 10 seconds?

### Offer Clarity

- What are the main ways people work with Theo right now?
- What are the names of those offers, if any?
- What is the price range or starting point?
- Which offers are ready to be public now?
- Which ideas should stay private or exploratory?

### Client Pathway

- Who is the right person for this work?
- Who is not ready for this work?
- What questions should people answer before they book?
- What signals show a client is ready?
- What signals show they need a slower or different path?

### Visual Vault

- Which images or collections might eventually be licensed?
- Are there images that should never be public?
- Should the vault feel private, editorial, commercial, artistic, or educational?
- What kinds of licensing does Theo imagine: personal use, editorial, commercial, exclusive, non-exclusive?
- Does Theo want photography lessons or visual identity education in the future?

## NotebookLM Podcast Prompt

Create a thoughtful, personal, and detailed audio overview for Theo about the Howling MUNE OS project.

The tone should feel warm, grounded, clear, premium, and human. Do not make it sound like a generic business podcast. Speak as if this is a private project briefing for a creator-healer who cares deeply about embodiment, photography, identity, healing, silence, beauty, and helping people return to themselves.

The purpose of the audio is to help Theo understand:

1. where the project started,
2. how it evolved,
3. why the work is now more than a landing page,
4. what the Client Journey System is,
5. what the Visual Vault could become,
6. what Google Drive is for,
7. what he needs to upload,
8. what decisions he needs to make,
9. how he can support the build with clear, honest feedback,
10. what the next stage looks like.

Explain the central diagnosis clearly: “The medicine exists. The pathway around the medicine does not yet exist clearly enough.”

Explain that the system is being built to protect the intimacy and depth of Theo's work, not flatten it into a generic sales funnel.

Explain the two pillars:

- Client Journey System: for people who want to work with Theo.
- Visual Vault: for photography, image licensing, private archives, visual identity work, future lessons, and creative assets.

Explain the desired website feeling:

- moonlit sanctuary
- living moon
- slow movement
- editorial typography
- dark, cinematic, quiet, premium
- not loud, not generic, not overly commercial

Make it clear that Theo does not need to perfectly organize everything. He should use the Google Drive folder called `10_UPLOAD_HERE_FIRST` as the intake station and place photos, videos, voice notes, copy, references, testimonials, and ideas there.

Ask Theo reflective questions throughout the podcast, including:

- What words feel most true to the work?
- What public claims should we avoid?
- Which offers are ready to be visible?
- What should remain private for now?
- Who is truly ready for this work?
- What should the Visual Vault become first?
- Which images are sacred/private versus licensable/public?
- What would make the next 30, 60, and 90 days successful?
- What does Theo need the system to protect?

End the audio with a clear request:

Theo should listen, then upload assets into Google Drive, answer the open questions in notes or voice notes, and give specific feedback on the website direction so the build can move into the next stage.

Keep the audio intimate, direct, and respectful. The goal is not to overwhelm Theo. The goal is to focus him, help him see the vision, and show him exactly how to participate.
